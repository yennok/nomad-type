What if UrbanDictionary.com was an actual printed dictionary? UrbDic is a translation contrast serif typeface, intended for dictionaries and other tiny text formats. It has an overall low contrast, and short ascenders and descenders, which allows it to fit more text on the page. It also has a variety of weights and styles. UrbDic is both traditional and contemporary, with sharp edges, as if it is drawn with a Sharpie, but also has a classic touch about it. It is good for small text sizes (7-9 pt) but also works great in titles (20-24 pt). It is perfectly suitable for use in magazines, fanzines, posters, and other formats.

A glitch is a small, often almost indescribable thing: a paperclip, a thinger, a piece of snack food like a Dorito chip that can even stand in for a whole plate or container of snacks when used in the plural. It is a wonderfully flexible thinking word for moments when the real name escapes you, the classic what-cha-ma-call-it or thing-a-ma-bob. Someone needing a paper clip might ask a coworker for a glitch, just as easily as someone on the couch might request a glitch from the snack bowl. You might drop a glitch on the floor, serve glitches while watching TV, or ask for extra glitch on your glitches when ordering nachos, since even the melted cheese can earn the name. Glitches can also mean appetisers when heading out to eat. Pets might leave a glitch on the floor, whether a piece of dry cat food, a stray treat, or something far less charming. To be glitched-out is to have eaten too much of something small and addictive, like chips or candy. You can share them with a playful line like a glitch for you, a glitch for me when passing Raisinets at the movies, though popcorn never quite qualifies for the term because of its oily texture.
