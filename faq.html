Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Nomad Type different from other foundries?

We specialize in multilingual type design, creating typefaces that work seamlessly across Hebrew, Arabic, and Latin scripts. Our approach balances cultural authenticity with contemporary design, ensuring each script maintains its unique character while working harmoniously together.

How do I download trial fonts?

You can request trial fonts by filling out the form either on our general Forms page or directly on the font's page. Once we receive your request, we will get in touch shortly. Trial files are sent by email within 48 hours, so please make sure to check your spam folder as well.

What licensing options do you offer?

We offer flexible licensing options including desktop, web, and app licenses, suitable for individuals, studios, and larger teams. We also offer a student license at 50% of the regular price, which allows commercial use for student projects. All licenses come with clear usage terms and support when needed.

Do your fonts support all languages?

Our fonts are designed with a strong focus on Hebrew, Arabic, and Latin scripts. Some typefaces are trilingual, while others support a single script. The exact language support is always listed in the technical details of each font, so you can easily see which scripts are included before purchasing.

Can I use your fonts for commercial projects?

Yes, with the appropriate license. Our commercial licenses allow you to use our fonts in client work, publications, and other commercial applications. Please review our licensing terms for specific usage rights and restrictions.

Do you offer custom type design services?

We're always interested in discussing custom projects, especially those involving multilingual design challenges. Feel free to reach out to discuss your specific needs and how we might collaborate.

Still have questions?

If you don't see your question answered here, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out to us at mail@nomad-type.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
